# Validity ranges and declined terms

This page is
[`docs/stopping-models.md`](https://github.com/2AMLogic/lindhard/blob/main/docs/stopping-models.md),
included verbatim so there is one copy. It collects the validity ranges of
the electronic stopping models side by side, and records the terms that are
deliberately not implemented and why (the clean-room rules exclude
coefficient sets that are only published as tables). The model pages are
[Lindhard-Scharff](stopping-lindhard-scharff.md),
[Oen-Robinson](stopping-oen-robinson.md),
[Bethe-Bloch](stopping-bethe-bloch.md),
[user tables](stopping-user-tables.md), [Bragg additivity](stopping-bragg.md)
and [straggling](straggling.md); the references cited below are listed in
full on those pages and at the end of this one.
